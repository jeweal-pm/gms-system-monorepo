## Context

The owner wants a Sale page that looks like the other transaction documents and takes items from two buttons, **From Reserve** (items held by an approved Reserve) then **From Stock** (own stock from Load to Stock, and Memo Stock).

Facts from the checkout (2026-11-18, branch `feat/18-11-26-sale`):

- `backend-api-inventory` exists (Hono, schema `inventory`, ADR-0052 phase 1 implemented): Load to Stock, `inventory.stock` with buckets `reserved/memo_out/sold/consumed`, `stock_hold`, `stock_movement`, the inventory → master saga (`master_sync_op`, `SyncDriver`, 60 s sweep, `sync:redrive`), Approve Center endpoints, notification feed + SSE, `@gms/storage` photos under `files/load-to-stock/`. Nothing reads or consumes own stock yet.
- Reserve (`partner.reserve*`) and Memo Stock (`partner.memo_stock`) are owned by `backend-api-master`. Reserve Approve moves Memo Stock `pending → reserved` through `takeFromMemoStock`; **Memo Out shares the `reserved` bucket**. `reserve_line` has no sold quantity. `memo_stock` CHECK: `pending = received − returned − sold − reserved`, `0 ≤ purchased ≤ sold`. `sold` has no writer yet; Purchase › Sold group and Memo Outstanding › Vendor Sold already read it.
- Document Setup key `sale` (code `SL`), auth leaf `sale` and the sidebar slug exist. The master `MasterMenu` type and both approval registries lack `sale`. Reserve's "used" rule says "never used until a Sale page exists".
- The owner-selected boundary (SA skill; ADR-0052 D1; Q-LS-36 option A "final") places **Sale** in `backend-api-inventory`, together with Account Outstanding and the Sale Report that will read Sale data.

## Decision

**D1 — Placement.** Sale is the module `src/modules/sale/` of `backend-api-inventory` (Compose/DNS `backend-api-inventory`, port 3301, public base `/api/inventory/v1/sale`), owning tables in schema `inventory`. ADR-0052 D1 is **followed, not amended**. Master stays the only writer of Reserve and Memo Stock; inventory reaches them only through master's internal API (service token `INVENTORY_MASTER_SERVICE_TOKEN`, existing). No new container, network, secret or DB role. The ui-spec alternative (Sale in master for phase 1) is costed in Alternatives and offered as owner question SQ-SL-01.

**D2 — Data model (inventory).** `inventory.sale` (Reserve header shape: customer + shipping/invoice address ids and snapshots, `currency_code`, `exchange_rate` (Selling Rate, required when the currency is not the company currency), ref, note ≤ 250, discount/tax/labor/charges and totals, ADR-0050 approval columns, ADR-0045 soft-delete columns, `provisional`, `sync_state`, `applied_revision`, `version`), `inventory.sale_line` (`source` `RESERVE` | `STOCK`; `OWN_STOCK` added in phase 2), `inventory.sale_charge`, `inventory.sale_line_photo`, and the saga table `inventory.sale_sync_op`. One additive migration. Schema: [database-diagram](../apps/backend/inventory/sale/database-diagram.md).

**D3 — Lines.** Pick-only; every line names exactly one reference: a `RESERVE` line names a Reserve line (`reserve_line_id`, and through it the Memo Stock item); a `STOCK` line names a Memo Stock item (`memo_stock_id`). A reference appears once per document. Pcs/weight follow the existing stock-line rule (pcs or weight > 0, each ≤ the cap), Sale Price > 0; item fields, Stock ID, source document number and caps are copied server-side from master at Save. Save checks against current open/pending **without locks and holds nothing**.

**D4 — Reservation consumption (master).** `partner.reserve_line` gains `sold_pcs` / `sold_weight_ct` (default 0, CHECK `0 ≤ sold ≤ line qty`, manual lines always 0). Open = qty − sold. From Reserve lists open lines of **APPROVED, not deleted Reserves of the Sale's customer in the Sale's currency** (Q-SL-05). At apply, master moves `reserve_line.sold += q` and Memo Stock `reserved −= q, sold += q`. A Memo Out hold is never consumed because consumption always goes through a specific Reserve line's open quantity. The Reserve stays APPROVED; a line with open 0 leaves the picker; Reserve detail returns `soldPcs/soldWeightCt` per line (display only). A Reserve with any sold line is **used** (ADR-0050 D4 amendment): its auto-approved edit/delete is refused with `RESERVE_IN_USE`. Release of the unsold remainder stays a later Reserve revision (Q-RS-03).

**D5 — Memo Stock consumption.** A `STOCK` line moves Memo Stock `pending −= q, sold += q` with the same id-ordered `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` lock and guard as `takeFromMemoStock` (new bucket `sold` in its fixed SQL map). Effects: Purchase › Add Memo Stock › **Sold** group shows `sold − purchased`; Memo Outstanding › Vendor **Sold** column fills and **Reserve** drops for From Reserve sales; Purchase Outstanding balance (`received − returned − purchased`) is unchanged until the vendor is paid; Memo Outstanding › Customer (Memo Out lines) is unaffected; Memo Receive becomes "used" (bucket rule, existing).

**D6 — Stock moves only when applied; one idempotent master call.** Applied = Approve (approval ON) or Save/Edit/Delete of an auto-approved Sale (approval OFF). Inventory applies through `PUT /internal/v1/inventory/sale-consumptions/{saleId}` with **set semantics** (`revision`, request hash, the full desired list of `{lineId, source, reserveLineId?, memoStockId, pcs, weightCt}`; an empty list releases everything), driven by the ADR-0052 D4 local saga (`sale_sync_op`, `SYNCING`, re-drive on access/start/60 s sweep ≤ 20 ops, `sync:redrive`). In **one master transaction**: upsert `partner.sale_consumption` (header, `FOR UPDATE`), lock the Reserve headers `FOR SHARE` (id order), the Reserve lines `FOR UPDATE` (id order) and the Memo Stock items `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` (id order), check each reference (Reserve APPROVED, not deleted, same customer and currency; Memo Stock exists), compute the delta against the previous applied set, re-check open/pending for increases and `sold − purchased ≥ release` for decreases, update the buckets, replace `partner.sale_consumption_line`, store the result. Same revision + same hash replays; lower revision or changed hash → `SALE_CONSUMPTION_STALE`. Short → `409 SALE_CONSUMPTION_EXCEEDS_AVAILABLE` (per line, nothing changed); unavailable reference → `422 SALE_CONSUMPTION_LINE_UNAVAILABLE`; a release below `purchased` → `409 SALE_CONSUMPTION_IN_USE`. No HTTP call runs while a transaction is open. The inventory `SyncDriver` is generalised over an op store + master port so Load to Stock and Sale share it without behaviour change for Load to Stock.

**D7 — Approval (ADR-0050).** New approval type `sale` stored in `inventory.approval_setting` (default ON), read `FOR SHARE` in Save/Submit; Approval Setup reads/writes it through inventory (ADR-0052 D8 pattern). Save → DRAFT (ON) or auto-approved applied (OFF, provisional create as Load to Stock); Send to approve → PENDING_APPROVAL; Approve / Revise (once) / Reject through inventory approval endpoints; Edit/Delete only in DRAFT / REVISION_REQUESTED, or **auto-approved and not used** (SQ-SL-02). Sale **used** = releasing any of its quantities would push a Memo Stock item's `sold` below `purchased` (a Purchase has paid the vendor for it); later Receivable/return modules add their rule. Restore (ADR-0045) of a deleted Sale returns it as DRAFT with nothing applied (SQ-SL-03). No un-approve of an approval-ON Sale (Q-SL-16).

**D8 — Approve Center and notifications.** Inventory `GET /approval/summary` and `/approval/pending?type=sale` with master shapes (party = customer, value = grand total in document currency, qty totals); preview = `GET /sale/{id}`. Notification events `documentType: "sale"` in the inventory feed (approvers of leaf `sale` on Send to approve; creator on Approve / Revise / Reject), written in the status-change transaction; existing inventory SSE stream, ids only (ADR-0049, ADR-0052 D12). No central notification service or broker.

**D9 — Photos.** Sale lines have ≤ 5 own photos (ADR-0048: JPEG/PNG ≤ 5 MiB, signature check) via `@gms/storage` local-fs driver under scope **`files/sale/`** in inventory's existing storage root; metadata `inventory.stored_file` (`source_type = 'sale-line'`) owned by the Sale module; links in `sale_line_photo`. Upload needs `sale` ADD or EDIT; read needs `sale` VIEW and a link from a Sale line (or an unlinked file the caller uploaded). Bytes and metadata are **retained permanently**: removing a photo in the UI, replacing it, editing or deleting the Sale only unlinks it; no expiry or purge (ADR-0030). S3 remains a proposed driver only. Nginx needs one location `/api/inventory/v1/sale/photos` with a 6 MiB body cap (infra requirement INF-SL-001). Source-line photos are not copied (Q-SL-12 default).

**D10 — Numbering and master data.** Numbers from Document Setup key `sale` through the existing internal `document-numbering/resolve` (allow-list widened from `load-to-stock` to `load-to-stock | sale`), running number in inventory under an advisory lock (ADR-0052 D10). Options (customers with addresses, currencies, company currency, charge types, stone options) come from one master internal read; Save validates customer, addresses, currency and all references in **one** `POST /internal/v1/inventory/sale/prepare` call. The browser keeps reading the Selling Rate from master `currency.options` and the Memo Stock picker from master `memoStock.list`, both newly granted to `sale:VIEW`.

**D11 — Own stock (phase 2, not in this revision).** Prerequisites: ADR-0052 D15 phase 2a read `GET /api/inventory/v1/stock/available` (adds `sale:VIEW`), a Sale UI revision 2 for the "Our stock / Memo Stock" sections, and an SA revision 2. Design intent: `OWN_STOCK` lines consume `inventory.stock` `on_hand → sold` **locally** in tx0 under `FOR UPDATE` (id order) before the master call, reversed in tx1′ on a master refusal; price prefill = Load Sale Price (company currency) ÷ Selling Rate, editable. A From Reserve line whose Reserve holds own stock (phase 2b) must reduce that Reserve's `stock_hold` in inventory and its master Reserve line in one apply — to be designed with phase 2b.

**D12 — Capacity.** No new container, process, cache, worker or package. Inventory limits unchanged (0.20 vCPU / 256 MiB / heap 160 MiB / pool 5 + authz 1); master unchanged. Bounds: ≤ 200 lines and ≤ 20 charge types per Sale, pickers/lists ≤ 50 per page keyset, master consumption locks ≤ 200 Reserve lines + ≤ 200 Memo Stock rows in id order in one transaction, master calls: Save ≤ 2 (prepare + numbering on first save), apply 1, picker page 1, all 5 s timeout on the shared ≤ 4-socket client; sweep ≤ 20 ops/60 s shared with Load to Stock; photo transfers ≤ 3 per process (shared limiter). Compose total stays **1.75 vCPU / 2880 MiB**.

## Alternatives

| Alternative | Why not chosen |
|---|---|
| **Sale in `backend-api-master` for phase 1** (ui-spec recommendation) | Cheapest phase 1: one local transaction, reuses `CustomerStockDocument` (totals, party, approval, soft delete, photos) and the master approval registry, no saga. But it contradicts the owner-selected boundary (ADR-0052 D1, Q-LS-36 "final"), puts Sale Report / Account Outstanding (inventory modules) behind a cross-service read of Sale data, and own stock (phase 2) would still need the master → inventory hold saga. Moving Sale data later would be a cross-schema data migration. Offered to the owner as SQ-SL-01; choosing it amends ADR-0052 D1 and needs SA revision 2 (tRPC contract in master). |
| Inventory writes `partner` through a granted role or a master `SECURITY DEFINER` function | Atomic, but a cross-schema write / shared transaction across owners (forbidden by the boundary rule). |
| Consume the shared `reserved` bucket without a per-line sold quantity | Could sell a Memo Out hold or another Reserve's hold; Reserve could not show what is sold or become "used". |
| Hold stock at Save | Owner pattern is "moves at Approve" (ADR-0050); a hold at Save needs reversal on every draft edit. |
| Copy Load to Stock's `master_sync_op` (FK to `load_to_stock`) for Sale ops | A second table with the same columns is clearer than a polymorphic FK; the driver code is shared instead (D6). |
| Merge dialog for the reserved + pending parts of one Stock ID (Purchase ADR-0043 style) | Not requested; two rows with their own source line are enough (Q-SL-08). |

## Consequences and capacity

- **Master (`backend-api-master`), additive migration:** `reserve_line.sold_pcs/sold_weight_ct` + CHECKs + partial open-line index; partial index `reserve_open_customer_idx`; tables `partner.sale_consumption`, `partner.sale_consumption_line`; `MasterMenu` gains `sale`; `memoStock.list` and `currency.options` accept `sale:VIEW`; `reserve.get` lines gain `soldPcs/soldWeightCt`; Reserve "used" rule; `BUCKET_SQL.sold` / `RELEASE_SQL.sold`; new internal routes under `/internal/v1/inventory/sale*`; numbering allow-list `sale`. No backfill (all sold = 0).
- **Inventory (`backend-api-inventory`), additive migration:** Sale tables, `sale_sync_op`, `approval_setting` CHECK + row `('sale', true)`; generalised `SyncDriver`; new module, routes and photo scope.
- **Frontend:** a REST adapter for `/api/inventory/v1/sale` instead of the assumed master tRPC names in the UI data-fetching table (same hooks); approval type `sale` read from inventory in Approval Setup and Approve Center; bell unchanged (inventory feed exists).
- **Infra:** one nginx location (photos, 6 MiB). No Compose, image, secret, network or volume change. **DevOps:** no build-context change; existing inventory/master pipelines cover it.
- **Rollback:** disable the Sale route/menu; migrations are additive. Applied sales must be released (Delete of auto-approved sales) before dropping master columns; approval-ON sales cannot be un-applied (no reversal, Q-SL-16), so schema rollback after the first applied Sale is forward-fix only.
- **Risk:** the saga adds states; mitigated by the shared, already-tested driver plus Sale idempotency, kill-between-steps and concurrent-approve tests ([testing](../apps/backend/inventory/sale/testing.md)). Production RSS of inventory under a 200-line apply must be measured (shared with BE-LS-012).

## Acceptance and approval

- Observable checks (IT-SL-B* in [testing](../apps/backend/inventory/sale/testing.md)): approve a Sale with one From Reserve and one From Stock line → Reserve line sold += q, Memo Stock reserved → sold and pending → sold, Purchase Sold group lists both; partial sale leaves the rest open/pending; two sales of the same Reserve line → one succeeds, the other `SALE_SOURCE_SHORT` with nothing changed; a Memo Out hold on the same item is never consumed; Reserve auto-approved edit after a sale → `RESERVE_IN_USE`; auto-approved Sale delete after its item was purchased → `SALE_IN_USE`; a killed process between master apply and tx1 recovers to one consistent state; master replay of the same revision is idempotent; N+1 budgets met.
- Covers: this ADR; SA revision 1 bundle `document/apps/backend/inventory/sale/**` (decisions, architecture, api-contract, authorization, database-diagram, sequence-diagram, error-handling, dependencies, testing); the BE-SL / INF-SL / DO-SL rows in [task/todo/sale.md](../task/todo/sale.md); the amendment notes in ADR-0050 and ADR-0052. No new packages.
